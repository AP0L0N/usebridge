Disabling signs the person out everywhere and stops their API tokens. Their projects and usage stay; you can enable them again.

What differs by role

  • Regular user: nav is Portfolio and Usage only. Portfolio, boards and Usage show only their own projects and agents. /polymarket and /admin respond 404 (open question: 404 or 403).
  • Account menu (everyone): avatar initials + name, opens Account, API tokens & MCP, Log out.
  • Owner: adds Polymarket and Admin to the nav and an Owner chip in the top bar and account menu. Owner's Portfolio still shows only the owner's own projects.
  • Admin: lists users with role, projects, agents, last active; can disable and enable. It cannot delete accounts or open other users' boards (open question).
  • Mobile (≤720px): the nav collapses to the current page ▾ menu left of the avatar; the account name and Sample data chip are hidden, the avatar stays.
